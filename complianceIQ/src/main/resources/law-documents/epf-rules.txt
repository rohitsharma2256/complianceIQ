EPF (Employees Provident Fund) Rules 2025

EPF Contribution Rates:
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic salary plus dearness allowance
- Employer contribution: 12% of basic salary plus dearness allowance
- Of employer's 12%, 8.33% goes to Employee Pension Scheme (EPS)
- Remaining 3.67% goes to EPF account
- Administrative charges: 0.5% of wages, minimum Rs 500

EPF Applicability:
- Mandatory for establishments with 20 or more employees
- Employees earning up to Rs 15,000 basic must be enrolled
- Employees earning above Rs 15,000 can opt in voluntarily

EPF Deadlines:
- Monthly contribution due by 15th of following month
- ECR (Electronic Challan cum Return) filing by 15th
- Annual return by 30th April

EPF Penalties for Late Payment:
- 5% per annum for delays up to 2 months
- 10% per annum for delays of 2 to 4 months
- 15% per annum for delays of 4 to 6 months
- 25% per annum for delays above 6 months

Maharashtra Minimum Wages 2026:
- Zone I unskilled: Rs 13,921 per month
- Zone II unskilled: Rs 13,325 per month
- Zone III unskilled: Rs 12,728 per month
