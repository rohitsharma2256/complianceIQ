ESI (Employees State Insurance) Rules 2025

ESI Contribution Rates:
- Employee contribution: 0.75% of gross wages
- Employer contribution: 3.25% of gross wages
- Total ESI contribution: 4% of gross wages

ESI Applicability:
- Applicable to employees earning gross wages up to Rs 21,000 per month
- For persons with disability, the limit is Rs 25,000 per month
- Mandatory for establishments with 10 or more employees
- Once covered, employee remains covered even if salary crosses limit during contribution period

ESI Contribution Periods:
- First period: April to September
- Second period: October to March
- Salary limit checked at start of each contribution period

ESI Benefits:
- Medical benefit for employee and family
- Sickness benefit: 70% of wages up to 91 days
- Maternity benefit: 26 weeks paid leave
- Disablement benefit
- Dependents benefit in case of death

ESI Deadlines:
- Monthly contribution due by 15th of following month
- Return filing twice a year

ESI Penalties for Late Payment:
- Interest at 12% per annum on delayed contribution
- Damages ranging from 5% to 25% depending on delay period
- Delay up to 2 months: 5% damages
- Delay 2 to 4 months: 10% damages
- Delay 4 to 6 months: 15% damages
- Delay above 6 months: 25% damages
