TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Rules on Salary 2025-26

New Tax Regime Slabs for FY 2025-26:
- Income up to Rs 3,00,000: Nil
- Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 7,00,000: 5%
- Rs 7,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 10%
- Rs 10,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 15%
- Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 15,00,000: 20%
- Above Rs 15,00,000: 30%

Standard Deduction:
- Rs 75,000 for salaried employees under new regime
- Rs 50,000 under old regime

Section 87A Rebate:
- Full rebate if taxable income up to Rs 7,00,000 under new regime
- Effectively no tax up to Rs 7,00,000 income

TDS Calculation on Salary:
- Estimate annual salary income
- Subtract standard deduction
- Apply applicable slab rates
- Divide annual tax by 12 for monthly TDS

TDS Deadlines:
- Monthly TDS payment due by 7th of following month
- For March, due by 30th April
- Quarterly TDS return (Form 24Q) filing required
- Q1 by 31 July, Q2 by 31 October, Q3 by 31 January, Q4 by 31 May

TDS Forms:
- Form 24Q: Quarterly TDS return for salary
- Form 16: Annual TDS certificate to employee (by 15 June)

TDS Late Payment Penalty:
- Interest at 1.5% per month on delayed TDS
- Late filing fee Rs 200 per day under Section 234E
- Penalty under Section 271H up to Rs 1,00,000
