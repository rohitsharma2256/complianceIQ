Professional Tax Rules by State 2025

Professional Tax is a state-level tax on income from profession, trade or employment.

Maharashtra Professional Tax Slabs:
- Monthly salary up to Rs 7,500: Nil
- Rs 7,501 to Rs 10,000: Rs 175 per month
- Above Rs 10,000: Rs 200 per month (Rs 300 in February)
- Annual maximum: Rs 2,500

Karnataka Professional Tax Slabs:
- Monthly salary up to Rs 25,000: Nil
- Above Rs 25,000: Rs 200 per month

Tamil Nadu Professional Tax:
- Levied half-yearly based on income slabs
- Maximum Rs 2,500 per year

West Bengal Professional Tax Slabs:
- Monthly salary up to Rs 10,000: Nil
- Rs 10,001 to Rs 15,000: Rs 110 per month
- Rs 15,001 to Rs 25,000: Rs 130 per month
- Rs 25,001 to Rs 40,000: Rs 150 per month
- Above Rs 40,000: Rs 200 per month

Telangana Professional Tax Slabs:
- Monthly salary up to Rs 15,000: Nil
- Rs 15,001 to Rs 20,000: Rs 150 per month
- Above Rs 20,000: Rs 200 per month

States Without Professional Tax:
- Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan do not levy professional tax

Professional Tax Deadlines:
- Monthly payment generally by 10th or as per state rules
- Annual return filing required
